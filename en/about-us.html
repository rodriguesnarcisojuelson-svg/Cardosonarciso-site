About Us

More than 10 years supporting healthcare in Angola.

Our track record

Built in the field, hospital by hospital.

With more than 10 years of experience in medicinal gases and hospital equipment in Angola, Cardoso Narciso has built its track record alongside hospitals, provincial governments and international institutions, providing reliable technical solutions and close support throughout the country.

We have worked in 10 of Angola’s 21 provinces and more than 30 municipalities, including on a UNDP (United Nations) programme, and we maintain an official representation agreement with Ultra Controlo.

Mission

"Cardoso Narciso contributes to the modernisation of the National Health System by providing technical solutions that strengthen the autonomy, safety and efficiency of healthcare facilities in Angola."

Public sector

Commitment to public healthcare

We work continuously with public healthcare bodies in Angola, providing equipment, technical assistance and training that support the day-to-day operation of healthcare facilities in the field.

Private sector

Commitment to the private sector

We also support clinics and private operators, helping strengthen technical capacity and the quality of care across Angola’s private healthcare sector.

Quality and compliance

A management system guided by international standards.

Our internal processes are developed on the basis of ISO 9001 (quality management), ISO 14001 (environmental management) and ISO 13485 (medical devices), as part of an ongoing implementation process that reflects our commitment to continuous improvement.
